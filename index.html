What We Do

Truck & Trailer Repair You Can Count On

One shop, six specialties. Whether it’s a five-minute oil change or a full transmission rebuild, our technicians handle it under one roof.

Preventive Maintenance

Oil and filter changes, lubrication, brake inspections, cooling system checks and belt or hose replacement to catch small issues before they become breakdowns.

Clutch Repair & Replacement

Clutch inspection, adjustment, repair, flywheel inspection and complete clutch replacement. A slipping clutch can take a truck off the road fast — we diagnose it right the first time.

Transmission Repair

Transmission diagnostics, service, repair and replacement, including manual transmission work and related driveline repairs.

Differential & Driveline

Differential inspection, repair, service and noise diagnosis, plus driveline and drivetrain component repairs.

HVAC — Heating & Air Conditioning

Heating repairs, air conditioning service, blower and fan repairs, and HVAC troubleshooting so your cab stays comfortable in every Winnipeg season.

Electronic Diagnostics

Check-engine and electronic fault diagnosis, engine computer diagnostics, sensor and electrical troubleshooting, and warning-light diagnosis — using diagnostic equipment to find the real problem and reduce unnecessary parts replacement.
